# Zubaida's Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business. We plan the campaign, write the emails, and keep everything in one place. The studio does not send any email.

## Folders

- `brand/`: the business's voice, tone, colours, logo notes and anything else that keeps emails on-brand.
- `leads/`: notes about who we are writing to, such as customer groups and audience details.
- `campaigns/`: one folder per campaign, holding its plan and its email drafts.
- `notes/`: research, decisions and anything else worth remembering.

## Brand voice

Follow the brand voice guide in every email and every piece of copy:

@brand/voice.md

## Rules

1. **Write in plain English.** Use short sentences and everyday words, and avoid jargon. This applies to the emails and to anything you write to me.
2. **Never send email to real people.** Only draft emails. Don't send anything, schedule anything, or connect to a sending service in a way that could reach a real inbox.
3. **Ask me before deleting anything.** That includes files, folders, drafts and lead lists.
