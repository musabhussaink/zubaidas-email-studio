# Zubaidas welcome series plan

**Brand:** Zubaidas (https://zubaidas.com/)
**Campaign:** A welcome series for people who join the Zubaidas email list
**Length:** each new subscriber gets 6 emails over their first 4 weeks (day 0 to day 24)
**Suggested build dates:** Monday 16 November to Sunday 29 November 2026, live from Monday 30 November 2026
**Channel:** email only, no paid ads, no discount or offer
**Email platform:** Mailtrap Email Testing (sandbox) for now. Built so Klaviyo or another production platform can be added later.
**Status:** draft plan and draft emails. Nothing is sent or scheduled from this studio.

> **Practice project.** This is an educational exercise, not a campaign approved by Zubaidas. We have no access to Zubaidas' internal information. All facts come from the public website and are recorded, with sources, in [`facts.md`](facts.md). Anything we couldn't verify is a `[PLACEHOLDER]` or `[CONFIRM]` in the drafts. We test only with the synthetic profiles in [`leads/test-subscribers.csv`](../../leads/test-subscribers.csv).

> **How this differs from a launch campaign:** a welcome series isn't sent to the whole list on set dates. It runs on its own in your email platform, and each person starts it on the day they sign up. So the "4 weeks" is each subscriber's first 4 weeks with us, and the calendar in section 5 counts days from sign-up.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Name:** *Welcome to the family*

**In one sentence:** Over their first four weeks, we help each new subscriber feel at home with Zubaidas, show them products that fit their stage of parenthood, and make their first order easy.

**Main goal:** 5% of new subscribers place their first order within 28 days of signing up.

This is a starting guess. We don't have past Zubaidas email numbers yet. After the first full month, we replace it with a target based on real results.

**Other goals:**
- Learn each subscriber's stage (expecting, new baby, toddler or older, or gift buyer), so every later email can be more useful.
- Build trust before we ask for a sale, by explaining honestly how ordering, delivery and returns work.
- Keep unsubscribes low, so people stay on the list for later campaigns.

**No offer:** there's no welcome discount. The series has to earn the first order through helpful advice, clear information and good product picks. If you decide to add a welcome offer later, sections 3, 5 and 8 need updating.

## 2. Target audience

Anyone who has just joined the email list. They chose to hear from us, so they're interested, but most haven't bought yet. They're at the **consideration stage**: they want to know if Zubaidas is a shop they can trust.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What might stop them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Expecting parents** | Preparing for a baby, often for the first time | A simple list of what they really need, and help choosing big items like a stroller, cot or car seat | Too many choices, worry about buying the wrong thing, budget pressure |
| **New parents (0–12 months)** | Busy, tired, buying often | Everyday essentials (feeding, clothing, care) that are easy to reorder | No time, unsure about sizes, want to trust what they put near their baby |
| **Parents of toddlers and older kids** | Child is growing fast | Clothing in the next size, toys, items for the next stage | Kids outgrow things quickly, so value matters a lot |
| **Gift buyers** | Relatives and friends shopping for a baby shower, new baby or birthday | A thoughtful gift that's easy to choose and arrives on time | Don't know what the parents need, worried about sizes and delivery timing |

**Where they are:** their inbox, and on zubaidas.com where they signed up. Many will also read on their phone, so every email must be easy to read on a small screen.

**Before we start:** we probably don't know which group each person is in. The first email asks them to pick (see section 5). Anyone who doesn't pick gets the general version of each email.

## 3. Key messages

**Main message:** *Thoughtfully selected essentials for every stage of parenthood, from a shop that only tells you what's true.*

**Supporting messages:**

1. **We help you choose, not just buy.** Simple guides and checklists for each stage. *Proof needed: none for the advice itself, but every product fact we mention must match the product page or supplier documents.*
2. **Something special for your little one, at every stage.** Collections that grow with your child. *Proof needed: real collections and products on the website, in stock when the email is written.*
3. **Shopping with us is simple.** Clear delivery, payment and returns. *Proof needed: the real delivery charges, delivery times, cities covered, payment methods and return policy. We say nothing about these until they're confirmed.*
4. **Great value for everyday essentials.** *Proof needed: real prices. We never say "lowest prices" or "cheapest".*

**Message by group:**
- **Expecting parents:** calm and reassuring. "You don't need everything at once. Here's what really matters first."
- **New parents:** short and practical. Make reordering everyday essentials quick.
- **Toddlers and older kids:** cheerful and growing-together. Lead with what's new for the next stage.
- **Gift buyers:** helpful and easy. Lead with gift ideas and how delivery works.

**Claims we must not make** (from the voice guide):
- No "100% safe", "doctor recommended", "clinically tested" or health claims like "prevents colic" or "helps baby sleep".
- No "organic", "BPA-free", "non-toxic", "hypoallergenic", "certified", "original" or "imported" unless that exact product has documents to prove it.
- No "free delivery", "next-day delivery" or "nationwide delivery" unless the real terms say so.
- No "most loved", "bestseller" or "almost sold out" without real sales or stock figures.
- No "lowest prices in Pakistan", "number one" or "perfect for every baby".
- No guilt or fear, for example "Is your baby's bottle putting them at risk?"

## 4. Channel strategy

**Email only.** This is the right fit: these people asked to hear from us, the series runs on its own once it's built, and it costs very little to run.

| What | Why it fits | Format | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Signup form on zubaidas.com** | It's where people join, and it sets what they expect | Short form with a clear promise, for example "Baby-care tips and new arrivals, about once a week." Optional: ask their stage here. | Low |
| **Welcome series emails** | Reaches every new subscriber at the right moment | 6 short emails, one main message and one button each. Emails 2 and 4 have 4 versions, one per group. | Medium (build once, then it runs) |
| **Collection pages on the website** | Every email needs somewhere useful to send people | Use existing collection pages. No new landing page needed. | Low |

**Rules for the series:**
- **No more than 2 emails a week** to any one person.
- **When someone places their first order**, they skip the shop-focused email (email 5). They still get the helpful ones.
- **If someone is also getting a regular newsletter or sale email**, pause regular emails for their first 2 weeks, or check that the two don't land on the same day.
- **Every email has an unsubscribe link** and the clean footer from the voice guide.
- **Every link has a tag** showing which email and which group it came from, so we can see what works.
- **Use the first name only if we have it.** Otherwise, "Hi there,". Never guess a name.

| **3** | Day 17 | **E5: Thoughtfully selected for you** | Not yet ordered | A short, warm pick of products for their stage. Say "popular" only if real sales figures back it up. Soft, friendly button, no pressure. | To do |
| **4** | Day 24 | **E6: How can we help?** | Everyone | A friendly check-in. Point to support or a 2-question survey: what are you shopping for, and what would help? Invite replies **only if someone reads that inbox**. Explain what regular emails they'll get from now on. | To do |

**Order that matters:**
- Delivery, payment and return terms must be confirmed before E1 (it mentions what to expect) and E3.
- E1's stage buttons must be set up in the email platform before E2, because E2 depends on them.
- Products in E2, E4 and E5 must be checked for real stock and correct prices just before going live, and again at each monthly review.
- Support or WhatsApp details must be confirmed before E6.

**Room to adapt:** a welcome series runs for months, so leave space. If one group stands out at the monthly review, add an extra stage-specific email for them in week 3 or 4 instead of E5.

**Seasonal note:** the series goes live in winter. If you want, E2 for new parents and toddlers can mention warm clothing, but keep the series mostly timeless so it doesn't need rewriting every month.

## 7. Success metrics

**Main measure:** the share of new subscribers who place a first order within 28 days. **Starting target: 5%.**

**Other measures:**

| Measure | Why it matters | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Stage chosen in E1 | Shows how many people we can send tailored emails to | Clicks on the 4 stage buttons |
| Click rate per email | Shows which messages work | Email platform reports |
| Orders by group | Shows which group to focus on next | Group tag on each link |
| Unsubscribe rate per email | Warns us if an email feels pushy or unwanted | Email platform reports. Review any email that gets far more unsubscribes than the others. |
| Spam complaints | Protects our ability to reach inboxes | Email platform reports. Look into it right away if they go up. |
| Survey answers from E6 | Tells us what parents are looking for | Survey tool |

**When to check:**
- **Weekly for the first month:** a quick look for broken links, bounces or one email doing badly.
- **Monthly after that:** compare orders against the target, check product stock and prices in each email, and update the target once we have real numbers.

## 9. Risks and how to handle them

| Risk | What we'd do |
|---|---|
| **Few people pick their stage in E1.** | Ask again, more simply, at the end of E3. Make sure the general versions of E2 and E4 are still useful on their own. |
| **Products in the emails go out of stock or change price.** The series runs for months. | Check every product at the monthly review. Link to collections rather than single products where possible. |
| **We state a shop fact that isn't true** (delivery, returns, payment). | Don't write E3 until each fact is confirmed in writing. Review E3 whenever a policy changes. |
| **Emails feel too salesy without an offer.** | Keep E2, E4 and E6 mainly helpful. Only E5 is product-led, and it's skipped for people who already ordered. |
| **New subscribers get the series plus regular emails and feel flooded.** | Pause regular emails for their first 2 weeks, or keep to the 2-a-week limit. |
| **The 5% target is wrong**, because we have no past numbers. | Treat month one as a baseline. Set a real target at the 28 December review. |
