# Welcome series: facts sheet

> **Practice project.** This is an educational exercise, not a campaign approved by Zubaidas. We have no access to Zubaidas' internal information. Everything below comes from the public website, or is marked as a placeholder or an assumption.

**Checked on:** Sunday 15 November 2026, on https://zubaidas.com/
**Check again:** before building the series, and at every monthly review. Prices, stock and policies can change.

How to read the labels:
- ✅ **Verified:** stated on the official website. The source link is given.
- ⚠️ **Conflict or caution:** the website says it, but something about it is unclear or contradicts another page.
- 🔲 **Placeholder:** not published. Leave the `[PLACEHOLDER: ...]` in the drafts until someone confirms it.
- 💭 **Assumption:** our own working guess. Not a fact about Zubaidas.
