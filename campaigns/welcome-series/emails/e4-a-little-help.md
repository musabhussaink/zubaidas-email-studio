## Version A: Expecting parents. Choosing a stroller

**Subject:** 4 questions to ask before you choose a stroller
**Preview text:** A simple way to narrow down the choice.

Hi {{first_name}},

A stroller is one of the bigger buys before your little one arrives, and there are a lot to choose from. These four questions can help you narrow it down:

**1. Where will you use it most?**
Busy markets, malls, parks or mostly in and out of the car? Think about your everyday routes, not just special outings.

**2. Will it fit in your car and your home?**
Measure your car boot and the space by your door. Then compare those numbers with the folded size on the product page.

**3. Can you fold and lift it easily?**
You'll often be folding it with a baby in one arm. If you can, try folding a few in person before you decide.

**4. Do you want a car seat that clicks on?**
Some strollers come as a "travel system" with a matching car seat. Others are sold on their own. Check the product details to see which kind you're looking at.

## Version C: Toddlers and kids. Winter clothes that work for busy little ones

**Subject:** Dressing little ones for winter 🧸
**Preview text:** Easy layers for busy days.

Hi {{first_name}},

Toddlers are on the move all day, so their clothes need to keep up. A few simple tips for the cooler months:

- **Check the size chart.** Sizes vary between brands, so look at the measurements on each product page, not just the age.
- **Think in layers.** A few light layers are easy to add or remove as the day warms up or cools down.
- **Choose easy on, easy off.** Wide necks, front openings and simple fastenings make busy mornings quicker.
