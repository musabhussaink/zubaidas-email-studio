# Zubaidas brand voice

Zubaidas is an online shop in Pakistan for baby products, kids' clothing, nursery furniture, strollers, feeding essentials, toys and parenting products. We write for expecting parents, new parents, families with young children and anyone shopping for quality baby essentials.

Use this guide for every email. When in doubt, ask: does this sound like a caring friend who knows about babies, and is every claim in it true?

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Warm. Knowledgeable. Honest.**

- **Warm:** we sound like a caring friend who understands new parents. We're cheerful and reassuring, never stiff or formal.
- **Knowledgeable:** we guide and teach, and we don't just sell. Parents should feel confident about their choices after reading.
- **Honest:** we only say what we can prove. Trust matters more to us than a quick sale.

How this looks on the page:

- **Language:** simple, natural English. Add a familiar Urdu or Roman Urdu phrase now and then for a personal touch, but don't overdo it.
- **Greeting:** "Hi Ayesha," when we know the customer's first name. "Assalam o Alaikum," for selected campaigns where a warm cultural greeting fits. "Hi there," or "Hello," when we don't know the name. Never guess or invent a name.
- **Emojis:** a few gentle ones where they add warmth, such as 🍼 💛 🧸 👶 🌸. Never one in every sentence.
- **Exclamation marks:** no more than 2–3 in a whole email, and fewer is better. "Happy parenting!" counts.
- **Length:** short and easy to scan. Use a clear subject line, a short personal opening, one main message or offer, relevant products and one clear call to action. Longer emails are fine for parenting tips or baby-care guidance.
- **Respect:** parents are busy, so every email needs a clear purpose.

- Comfort and care
- Thoughtfully selected
- Happy parenting
- Growing together
- Discover our collection
- Something special for your little one

- Affordable, great value (instead of "cheap")

Prefer real recommendations and clear benefits over big promises.

## 3. Words we avoid

- **BUY NOW!!!** or any shouting in capital letters
- **Cheap.** Say "affordable" or "great value" instead.
- **"Hurry" or "you'll miss out"** unless there is a real deadline
- **"Last chance"** unless it really is the last chance
- **"You are missing out"**
- **"Guaranteed best" or "number one"** without proof
- **"Perfect for every baby"**
- **Guilt:** anything suggesting someone is a bad parent
- **Fear:** scary messages about children's health, safety or development
- **Pushy sales talk** and piles of exclamation marks

## 4. Claims we must never make

**The main rule: if we can't check a claim, we leave it out.** Claims are only allowed when backed by current, reliable proof, such as supplier certificates, manufacturer documents, checked product details, stock records or confirmed shop policies. Never assume.

**Safety and health**
- Never say a product is "100% safe" or risk-free.
- Never say "doctor recommended", "medically approved" or "clinically tested" without proof.
- Never say a product prevents colic, improves sleep, boosts development or has health benefits without reliable proof.
- Only make safety claims backed by the product's documents.

**Product facts**
- Never call a product organic, BPA-free, non-toxic, hypoallergenic, certified or eco-friendly unless that specific product has documents to prove it.
- Never invent materials, specifications, certifications or age ranges.
- Check every product fact against the real product information or supplier documents.

**Prices and delivery**
- Never claim "lowest prices in Pakistan" without proof.
- Never promise free, next-day or nationwide delivery unless the actual offer and delivery terms support it.
- Always use correct prices, offer terms, delivery charges and conditions.

**Stock and authenticity**
- Never call products original, authentic, imported or officially sourced unless we can prove it.
- Never invent scarcity, such as "almost sold out", without real stock figures.
- Never advertise a product as in stock when it isn't.

## 5. How we sign off

**Who signs:** always **The Zubaidas Team**, never one person's name.

**Closing lines (rotate these):**
- Happy parenting!
- With love and care,
- Wishing you and your little ones the best,
- Warm wishes,

"Allah Hafiz" is fine now and then for culturally fitting emails, but it isn't the default.

**Footer (keep it clean and friendly):**
- The Zubaidas Team
- Website: https://zubaidas.com/
- Customer support or WhatsApp link, only once the official details are confirmed
- Social media links, when relevant
- A link to unsubscribe from marketing emails (every marketing email needs this)

**Replies:** invite customers to reply with questions only if someone actually reads and answers that inbox.
